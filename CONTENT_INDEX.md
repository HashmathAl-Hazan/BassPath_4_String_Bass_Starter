# BassPath lesson index

Each lesson contains original teaching paragraphs, guided practice, an error/correction, practical checks and a knowledge question.

## Level 0: Instrument basics

- `l0-01` — Meet the bass and its job
- `l0-02` — Connect the bass and set a simple sound
- `l0-03` — Support the instrument and relax
- `l0-04` — Tune to E1–A1–D2–G2
- `l0-05` — Make a controlled fingerstyle note
- `l0-06` — Alternate index and middle fingers
- `l0-07` — Fret a note with light pressure
- `l0-08` — Stop notes and silence unused strings
- `l0-09` — Find the pulse and count four
- `l0-10` — Read four-string tablature
- `l0-11` — Listen for high and low
- `l0-12` — Complete the open-string milestone

## Level 1: Absolute beginner

- `l1-01` — Whole, half and quarter notes

- `l1-03` — Read your first bass-clef notes
- `l1-04` — Hear 3/4 and 6/8

- `l1-06` — Move the hand instead of forcing a stretch
- `l1-07` — Cross strings without losing time
- `l1-08` — Change volume and note length deliberately
- `l1-09` — Follow a four-chord root-note part
- `l1-10` — Listen to the kick drum
- `l1-11` — Follow sections and stop together
- `l1-12` — Record your first complete arrangement
